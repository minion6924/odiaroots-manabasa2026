About the Festival

Why Manabasa Gurubar?

Every Thursday of the holy month of Margasira, Odia households welcome Goddess Lakshmi with intricate jhoti chita rice-paste art, freshly made pitha and bhoga, and readings from the Lakshmi Purana. This competition invites Odia families across the United States to record and share these living traditions — so our children stay rooted, wherever they grow.

Make a Short Video

Film your family's Manabasa celebration — the jhoti, the pitha, the puja, the stories — up to 5 minutes.

Involve the Family

Children and multiple generations participating together score higher — this is about passing tradition on.

Win Heritage Awards

Winners in each of 8 categories receive a named heritage award and an "Award of Excellence" certificate.

Participation

How It Works

1

Register & Submit — One Form

Fill out the combined registration and submission form. Choose your competition category, give your video a title, and share it as a Facebook link or a Google Drive link.

2

Receive Your Entry ID

You'll instantly get a unique Entry ID (like ORM2026-0001) on screen. Save it — it's used for judging, results, and your certificates.

3

Judging

A panel of judges scores every entry on 6 weighted criteria: Cultural Authenticity (25%), Presentation Quality (20%), Explanation & Storytelling (20%), Family & Youth Participation (15%), Creativity (10%), and Overall Video Presentation (10%). People's Choice is decided by Facebook engagement instead.

4

Results & Certificates

Winners are announced per category on the Results page. Every submitted entry earns a "Keeper of Odia Tradition" participation certificate — and children who take part earn a "Young Keeper of Odia Culture" certificate too.

Compete In

The 8 Award Categories

Choose one category when you register. Entries compete only within their chosen category.

01

Best Overall Manabasa Presentation

Odia Roots Manabasa Heritage Champion

The most complete celebration — puja, jhoti, pitha, family, and storytelling together.

02

Best Traditional Jhoti Chita

Maa Lakshmi Jhoti Samman

Authentic rice-paste jhoti artistry — motifs, footprints of Maa Lakshmi, and finesse.

03

Best Pitha or Bhoga Presentation

Manabasa Pitha Parampara Award

Traditional pitha and bhoga — manda, kakara, chakuli, kheeri — made and presented with care.

04

Best Family Participation

Odia Heritage Family Award

Multiple generations celebrating together — grandparents to grandchildren.

05

Best Children's Presentation

Young Odia Heritage Ambassador

Children leading the celebration — explaining, performing, and carrying tradition forward.

06

Best Cultural Storytelling Video

Lakshmi Purana Heritage Voice

Narrating the Lakshmi Purana and the meaning of Manabasa Gurubar with heart.

07

Most Creative Presentation

Creative Odia Tradition Award

Fresh, imaginative ways of presenting tradition — editing, music, format, and originality.

08

People's Choice Award

Community Choice Award

Decided by genuine Facebook engagement (likes, meaningful comments, shares) — not judge scoring. Fake or paid engagement is disqualified.

Fair Play

Rules Summary

  • Open to Odia families residing in the United States. One entry per family per category.
  • Registration deadline: To Be Announced.
  • Videos should be roughly 2–5 minutes, filmed for this competition, and family-friendly.
  • Submit via a public Facebook link (tag the Odia Roots page) or a Google Drive link set to “Anyone with the link”.
  • Seven categories are scored by judges using the published weighted criteria; People's Choice uses genuine Facebook engagement only.
  • Paid promotion, bot activity, or artificial engagement leads to review or disqualification.
  • Judges' decisions are final. Entries compete only within their chosen category.
  • By consenting, you allow Odia Roots to feature your photos/video for community and cultural purposes.
